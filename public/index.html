How scoring works. Each signal scores 0 (green), 1 (amber) or 2 (red) against the thresholds shown under it. Pillar score = points earned ÷ points possible, counting only signals with data. Composite weights: credit 22, private credit 13, banks 13, breadth 13, labor 10, rates & funding 10, leaders 10, slow macro 5, volatility 4.
Data. Prices from Yahoo Finance (Stooq as backup), refreshed at most every 10 minutes during market hours and delayed about 15 minutes. Yields, spreads, funding, claims and jobs from FRED. Bank failures from the FDIC. Slow macro is maintained by hand in data/macro.json. Not investment advice.
